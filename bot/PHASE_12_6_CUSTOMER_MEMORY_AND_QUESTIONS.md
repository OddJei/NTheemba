# Phase 12.6 — Customer Memory and Question Analytics

Phase 12.6 records:

- business-private preferred names learned during checkout;
- business-specific delivery locations;
- compact terminal conversation summaries;
- business information and FAQ questions;
- answered, partial and unresolved outcomes;
- handover/follow-up requirements.

Raw conversation messages are stored only when `conversation_retention` consent is granted. Question analytics and summaries remain tenant-scoped and are never reused as another business's customer history.
