# Phase 11.14 — Multi-Conversation Simulator Workspace

The developer simulator now includes a workspace at:

```text
/dev/simulator/workspace
```

The workspace contains:

- a business-grouped conversation inbox;
- multiple independent customer sessions;
- a WhatsApp-style active chat;
- exact channel and adapter information;
- enabled canonical capabilities;
- pipeline traces;
- session and draft inspection;
- standard TradeFlow examples;
- Serah's customised client, product, booking, loyalty, and handover examples;
- cross-business customer-isolation examples.

Seeded businesses:

- Harvest Big Shop — standard TradeFlow;
- AMAC Enterprise — standard TradeFlow without delivery;
- Serah's Glow Lounge — customised TradeFlow capability set.

The old Phase 11.11 simulator endpoints remain available for regression compatibility.
