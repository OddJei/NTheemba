API SERVICE DESIGN: BUSINESS SERVICE

1. Description:
Manages MSME registration, business profiles, subscriptions, and contributor-linked metadata. It anchors MSME identity across the platform and links businesses to products, staff, delivery zones, and affiliate impact. Emits onboarding events for downstream automation and referral tracking. Integrates with Payment Service for subscription activation and renewal, and with Index/Search Service for discoverability.

4. Code Generation Strategy:

5. Routes:
POST   /business/register              → Register new MSME
GET    /business/{id}                  → Get business profile
PUT    /business/{id}                  → Update business details
POST   /business/{id}/subscribe        → Initiate subscription (calls Payment Service)
GET    /business/{id}/subscription     → Get subscription status
GET    /business/{id}/metadata         → Fetch business metadata for other services
POST   /business/reindex               → Re-publish all MSMEs to Index/Search Service

8. Scaling Readiness:
  - Single instance deployment with connection pooling
  - Indexed queries on `business_id`, `affiliate_id`, `referred_by_msme_code`
  - Basic background jobs for expiry and reminders

  - Stateless service design with Redis for pub/sub events
  - Horizontal scaling with Docker + Kubernetes
  - Partitioned subscription checks for faster expiry handling
  - Circuit breakers for Payment, Notification, and Index/Search Service calls

  - Sharded `businesses` and `msme_codes` tables (by region or business_id)
  - Event-driven architecture with Kafka/RabbitMQ for onboarding and subscription events
  - Read replicas for analytics queries
  - Autoscaling policies based on queue depth and subscription volume
  - Observability stack (Prometheus + Grafana) for proactive scaling
