## 🧩 Canonical Ingress Workflow (Mind‑Map)

1. **Adapter Intake** → raw payload arrives in `incoming:message`.  
2. **Validation** → check required fields (`request_id`, `to`, `from`, `meta.platform`).  
3. **Idempotence** → reject duplicates by `request_id`.  
4. **Normalization** → standardize phone numbers, platform IDs.  
5. **Forward to ICE** → validated payload passed to ICE.  
6. **ICE Hydration** → build blobs:  
   - Business + Owner blob (from `businesses` + `users`).  
   - Customer blob (from `users` keyed by phone).  
   - Session blob (from `sessions` + `session_state_cycles`).  
   - Affiliate blob (if affiliate_id present).  
   - Cart blob (from CartService).  
   - Order blob (from OrderDeliveryService).  
   - Product snapshot (from catalog/inventory).  
7. **Persistence** → hydrated payload persisted in PostgreSQL.  
8. **Ingress Completion** → enriched payload published to `bot:lane:{bot_type}`.  
9. **Audit Hooks** → emit `ingress_validated` or `ingress_rejected` events.  
10. **Error Handling** → malformed payloads → `ingress:deadletter`.

---

## 📝 Summary
- **Before enrichment** → raw adapter payload.  
- **After enrichment** → enriched payload with blobs (business+owner, customer, session, affiliate, cart, order, product snapshot).  
- **Ingress canonical workflow** → validate → deduplicate → normalize → ICE hydration → persist → publish enriched payload to bot lane.
